01

Meet your light

Measure the whole light: base, switches and any moving parts. This design stands around it, without attaching to it.

Use a photo to estimate dimensions

Take straight-on front and side photos. Put a ruler in the same plane as the feature measured. Perspective and hidden depth cause error; confirm every dimension physically.

Choose a photo, then mark 2 ruler endpoints and 2 opposite corners around the whole light.

Photos stay in memory on this device. They are not uploaded or included in saved projects. Keyboard/manual measurement fields are available below.
