CU.NGEN Lantern Studio 0.2.0

1. Extract the entire ZIP to a folder. Do not run from inside the ZIP preview.
2. Open index.html in a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari browser.
3. Read the prototype scope, choose your light type, and enter measurements.
4. Optional: open the photo measurement panel, choose a photo, mark two
   reference endpoints and two opposite corners of the light. A ruler is
   still required to confirm dimensions. This is NOT automatic 3D scanning.
5. Choose plain or split frame. Generate the design and review its holds.
6. Confirm supplier stock and quotes. Enter tools, shipping, tax, cutting
   and testing costs; blank costs remain unknown, never zero.
7. Save the project JSON, download the parts CSV and SVG, and use
   Download assembly booklet (HTML) for illustrated instructions. Open
   that file in your normal browser and print or save as PDF if desired.

No installation, server, account, paid API or internet is required for design.
Internet is needed only to visit suppliers or GitHub. Photos stay in memory;
they are not sent anywhere or saved in the project JSON. Save before closing.
On phones, local HTML/file handling differs by browser; desktop Chrome/Edge
is the intended first target. Browser tests used localhost; direct file
opening could not be automated. The camera/file picker depends on device support.

This version generates an independent tabletop surround with an open top
and bottom. It does not mount a shade to a bulb, modify wiring, handle hanging
fixtures, or certify a finished luminaire. Corded lights are mockup-only.
Hot bulbs and flame are unsupported. Physical joints, stability, material
temperature limits and safe operating conditions still require validation.

The booklet uses original assembly diagrams, not IKEA material. CU is not
affiliated with IKEA or any listed supplier. Catalog prices are dated, not
live delivered quotes. The selected wood listing may be unavailable.
