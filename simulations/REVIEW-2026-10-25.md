# Scan-to-kit review — October 25, 2026

This work adds a reproducible validation system around the existing 0.8 app. It does not train model weights or change the deployed app. No paid services or manufacturing were used.

## Results

- **56 automated tests passed**, including 12 additional complete quote journeys across three patterns, two stock choices and two fixture projections.
- **288 design combinations:** 234 generated and passed independent geometry checks; 54 rejected because the chosen borders or pattern could not fit. Rejections are not counted as passing designs.
- **14 synthetic image cases:** clean ellipses at three aspect ratios scored 0.9843–0.9910 intersection-over-union for the top outline candidate. The blank image produced no candidates. Simple clutter retained the same successful detection. All three low-contrast cases, all three occluded cases and the clipped case produced no candidates. These are known coverage gaps, not successful scans.
- Inches and millimeters both preserve the approximate 393.7 mm diameter / 228.6 mm projection reference and its planning-only status. These dimensions are not independently measured ground truth.
- **18 planar measurement cases** exercise the separate calibrated tabletop measurement module with exact coplanar points and deterministic one-/three-pixel perturbations. All six noise-free cases recover the expected dimensions exactly at the module's output precision. Perturbed cases show errors up to 12.9 mm. This is a sensitivity demonstration, not a statistical error bound, and it does not recover ceiling protrusion or validate non-coplanar measurements.
- A real desktop browser walkthrough of the current source completed manual approximate capture, Terrace selection, 30 mm side spacing, save, reload, restore and quote-package download. Checkout remained disabled. This was not an iPhone camera or LiDAR test.

The user’s existing localhost:8766 tab was still displaying **0.6** during inspection. The walkthrough used the current repository's **0.8** source on a separate temporary local server. An old local preview should not be mistaken for the current source or hosted deployment.

## What the checks establish

The independent auditor checks finite orthogonal contours, hole containment/separation, six-millimeter paper overlap, paper clear of joints, complete stock placement with eight-millimeter edge margins and five-millimeter part gaps, assembled corner and base-tab alignment, matching cut perimeter and unresolved manufacturing status. Mutation tests deliberately displace joints, remove paper, duplicate stock placements, shorten paper, falsify perimeter and change readiness to make sure bad designs fail.

The customer tests execute the actual UI handlers, unpack their ZIP outputs and compare panel SVGs, parts dimensions, paper sizes, stock count, quote amounts and unknown-price labels against the selected design. They verify that saving/restoring preserves refinements and that a quote request does not become an order.

These checks do not prove mechanical strength, assembly accessibility, material tolerances, thermal performance or mounting reliability. The present scope is circular fixture envelopes; irregular fixtures are not validated by this report.

## Cost finding

At the existing October 20 reference prices, your approximate nine-inch projection uses three square sheets and one paper roll for **$76.96 including the CU fee**, compared with **$114.37** for two long sheets and one paper roll. That is **$37.41 less in the known subtotal**, with the same panel geometry. It is not a final kit price: nine cost categories remain unquoted. Current stock availability and prices were not rechecked in this test run.

Quiet, Horizon and Terrace have equal material subtotals for this envelope, but panel cut perimeters differ: 16.859 m, 29.777 m and 26.036 m. Shorter perimeter may reduce cutting time; supplier speed, piercing, setup and minimum charges determine the actual cost. No cutting-time or complete-price saving is claimed.

The current packer is a shelf heuristic without rotation. The comparison selects between the tested stock options; it does not establish a globally optimal material layout.
