05 / Assembly

Tools: ruler, square, pencil, secured hand-saw miter box, suitable clamps, scissors, sandpaper and a protected flat surface. Cutting/handling costs are not assumed free.

1. Confirm the envelope

Unplug or switch off the original light. Measure the entire 80 mm × 80 mm × 140 mm envelope, including switches and moving parts. Make a cardboard mockup first; keep it unlit. Check manufacturer clearance guidance and the 25 mm bottom opening.

2. Cut and label

Use straight 12.7 mm square wood, a secured hand-saw miter box, a square and a protected table. Label A1–A4 uprights, B1–B4 front/back rails, C1–C4 side rails, D1–D4 center mullions. Cut to the table below, allowing for the saw kerf. Check paired lengths within 0.5 mm as a workmanship target.

3. Make two flat frames

Place A1/A2 parallel, outer edges 181.4 mm apart. Glue B1 flush with the top; B2 lower edge 25 mm above the feet. Rails butt between posts. Repeat with A3/A4 and B3/B4. Use Titebond Original, square and clamps; diagonals should agree within 1 mm. Make an offcut joint coupon first. Follow the glue label; do not stress joints for at least 24 hours.

4. Connect front to back

Join the two cured frames with C1–C4 between the inner post faces: two at the top and two at the same lower-rail level. Outer depth is 181.4 mm. Clamp square, check all four feet contact the table, and allow another full cure. Add one D mullion between the rails at the center of each face; its ends butt to the rails. Cure before papering. Reject open joints or visible racking. Carry only from below.

5. Fit the four paper panels

Cut two front/back panels 168 mm × 152.6 mm and two side panels 168 mm × 152.6 mm after measuring the real openings. Apply a thin line of Shoji Nori paste to the outside frame faces, leaving 6 mm overlap. Test a coupon for stain, wrinkle and peel. Keep the top and bottom open; paper is not a brace.

6. Dry-fit around the light

With the light OFF, lower the independent surround over it. Keep the light on its own original base. Do not attach the surround to the light or cover ventilation. Check controls, removal access, movement and clearance in every direction. If the light or cord touches anything, stop and revise. Corded sources are outside lit-use scope in this release.

7. Record prototype tests

Test the unlit frame for racking, tipping, sliding and loose paper. No strength or stability rating is established. Before a supervised lit trial, resolve manufacturer permission and material/component temperature limits, then measure against those limits. Record failures. No flame, hot bulbs, hanging or unattended operation. Software checks do not approve a physical lamp.
