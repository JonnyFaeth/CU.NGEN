Meet your light.

A small, complete tabletop LED light. The new frame stands around it; no wiring changes.

How to get a useful measurement
  1. Switch the light off. Include its base, controls and full outline.
  2. Put a flat rectangle with measured sides beside the light, in the same plane as the face being measured. A blank card or book works; no printer needed.
  3. Use the 1× camera straight on. Keep the whole reference visible.
  4. Mark its corners clockwise, then two opposite light corners. Repeat from the side for depth.
  5. Check all three dimensions with a ruler before fabrication. Curves, depth and lens distortion still introduce error.

This web app does not access the iPhone LiDAR sensor. It corrects a reference plane, not a whole 3D object.
