# Contributing

CU.NGEN is currently a public design project. Helpful contributions include sourced component comparisons, clear requirements, reproducible experiments, and corrections to assumptions.

1. Check existing proposals before starting overlapping work.
2. Explain the user problem, scope, cost impact, and alternatives.
3. Separate calculations, simulations, and physical measurements.
4. Include exact part/software revisions and a reproducible protocol when reporting tests.
5. Include failures and limitations. Do not claim reliability or compatibility from a demonstration alone.
6. Disclose substantial AI assistance and review all generated claims and code.
7. Keep private data, credentials, personal photos, and unrelated project files out of contributions.

Discuss mechanical architecture and new dependencies before large implementations. Maintainer review is required before adopting a design or purchasing parts on behalf of the project.

## Licensing

Read [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md). No project reuse license or contributor agreement has been selected. Do not import third-party code/CAD without identifying its license and permissions. We are not requesting copyright assignment.

## Conduct

Be respectful and focus criticism on evidence and design choices. Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information are not welcome. The maintainer may moderate project discussions.
