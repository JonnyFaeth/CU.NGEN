Reduce the earlier illustrative frame to **177.8 × 177.8 × 254 mm** (7 × 7 × 10 inches), using actual **12.7 mm** square stock. This fits letter paper and leaves nominal 12.7 mm base margin. Two nominal 6.35 mm plaques make a 12.7 mm base and 266.7 mm overall height. Received thickness may differ.

Rail length follows W−2s. Paper has nominal 6.35 mm overlap on each frame edge. Do not cut all the paper until the actual assembled openings have been measured. Base corners are rounded: confirm that all posts and bottom rails land entirely on the plaque, without overhang. If not, use a larger base; do not assume the nominal bounding square is solid wood.

Conservative wood layout with up to 3 mm kerf per cut:

- Rod A: three 254 mm posts (771 mm with kerfs).
- Rod B: one 254 mm post + four 152.4 mm rails (878.6 mm with kerfs).
- Rod C: four 152.4 mm rails (621.6 mm with kerfs); retain offcuts for joint tests.

Each 914.4 mm rod has end-trimming allowance under this layout. Measure and mark each cut separately; sawing two nominal 6 inch rails from a 12 inch stick would lose length to the kerf.

No power tools or workshop are required. A [Stanley 20-600 miter box and hand saw](https://www.stanleytools.ca/product/20-600/mitre-box-saw), or an equivalent fine-cut kit, provides a square-cut guide on a protected, firmly supported tabletop. Secure the box and workpiece. If cutting is undesirable, give this cut request to a local maker or supplier; service availability and fee need confirmation. This is not yet a no-tools mail-order kit.

## Joint and assembly selection

**Square butt joints bonded with Titebond Original**, with the full bottom rail ring bonded to the laminated base. No miters, holes, screws, dowel pins or printed connectors in this revision. This is a lightweight glued frame; no structural rating is claimed and the paper is not a structural brace.

1. Make an offcut rail-to-post joint with the intended stock and glue. Check fit and curing before making the frame. Poorly fitted end grain joints are a likely weak point; do not fill gaps with a thick blob of glue.
2. Dry-fit two front/back frames on a flat protected board with a square. Target paired rail lengths within 0.5 mm and frame diagonal difference within 1 mm as provisional workmanship goals, not supplier guarantees. Sand cut ends lightly if necessary.
3. Glue and hold the first two frames square with small clamps and protective pads. Tape can locate pieces but is not a substitute for adequate joint contact pressure. Follow the glue label. Titebond recommends 30–60 minutes clamping for unstressed joints and no stressing for at least 24 hours; staged assembly will span more than one day.
4. Connect the cured frames with the remaining rails, keeping the box square. Glue the two plaques face-to-face under flat distributed pressure. After curing, center and glue the bottom rail ring/posts to the base. Remove squeeze-out where paper will attach.
5. After full cure, inspect joints, gently test racking, and check that the base sits flat. Stop and revise if joints open or the frame racks. Pick it up by the base, not the top rails. This prototype is not a handle or load-bearing structure.
6. Fit paper with a thin controlled bead of Y7 paste on the exterior frame faces. Use the [supplier's paper-attachment method](https://www.eshoji.com/faq/how-to-attach-shoji-paper-using-glue/) and coupon results. Keep the top open. Do not use wood glue on replaceable paper panels.
7. Measure the actual puck, battery-installed mass and service-opening motion. Dry-fit it centered on the base facing upward. Use its supplied adhesive base only if the supplied instructions permit that orientation and the adhesive bonds to this surface. Keep the removable battery cover and lens accessible through the open top. Never glue the battery compartment shut or wrap the puck in paper.

## What must be verified before a lit trial

Reserve an **80 × 80 × 40 mm** central source volume for planning only; these are **not verified GE product dimensions**. With that reservation, nominal side clearance to the inside rails is 36.2 mm, or 35.2 mm after the example's 1 mm allowance. The 30 mm previous design target is geometric only and is not a thermal safety limit. If the received source exceeds the reservation or its instructions prohibit the arrangement, update the design before installation.

Read the supplied instructions, then establish actual component/material operating limits before testing the assembly. Record source, paper, wood and adhesive temperatures during a supervised trial and compare with documented limits. Manufacturer wording such as "cool to the touch" is not approval of a paper enclosure. No flame, incandescent bulb, improvised mains wiring or unattended use in this prototype. The light output through the shade, runtime and uniformity are unknown until measured.

The old generator example remains an illustrative 180 × 180 × 280 mm frame, **not this selected build**. Its mass and tipping outputs must not be applied here. This prototype uses rounded base corners, different dimensions and an unmeasured light source. Generate a new numerical model only after the source dimensions/mass and real base support geometry are recorded. Rounded-corner support and glued-joint strength are not modeled by v0.1.
