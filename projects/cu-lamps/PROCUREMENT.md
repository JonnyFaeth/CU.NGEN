The retrieved page showed shipping inventory but no selected Portland destination, and its displayed fulfillment dates were in the past. Treat inventory/dates as unconfirmed; select your actual store or delivery destination before ordering.

For the default 80 × 80 × 140 mm source, plain frame, the app requires three rods. Enter **2.49** in the wood unit-price override if that is your confirmed price. The listed material subtotal becomes **$23.85**, instead of $22.77. Shipping, tools, tax, cutting and testing are additional and remain unknown until entered. Keep the supplier URL/model in your saved purchasing notes; the 0.2.0 app's price override changes cost, not its supplier identity.

Measure stock before cutting: the app assumes 12.7 × 12.7 mm cross-section and at least 914.4 mm length. Reject bowed, split or undersized stock. A different cross-section needs regenerated joint geometry; do not substitute it solely by changing the price.

## First purchase should support an unlit mockup

Use the generated whole-unit bill of materials and cut list. Have the wood cut by a willing supplier or use a small hand saw/miter box on a secured protective board. This is not a tool-free build. You also need a ruler, square, clamps appropriate to the generated frame width, scissors/craft knife with cutting mat, and sandpaper. Check the manufacturer's glue instructions for clamping and cure time.

Before buying paper or approving an operating light, dry-fit the wood around the complete original light while it is off and cool. Check base, switches and motion clearance. Do not assume the generated default clearance is a thermal requirement from the light manufacturer. An unlit geometric mockup is the first milestone; an energized paper surround needs separate documented material, stability and thermal validation.

## Honest completion criteria

- The download and geometry/export software exist and have a published test record.
- The original light remains an existing complete product; this app does not sell or wire a new LED module.
- A fully delivered price still requires destination-specific supplier checkout quotes.
- Physical assembly instructions are prototype instructions; no real specimen has yet demonstrated repeatable assembly or safe operation.
- Automatic 3D reconstruction, ceiling-light mounting and a checkout integration are not present in 0.2.0.
