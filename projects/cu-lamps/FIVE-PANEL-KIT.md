# Five-panel ceiling lantern kit

Product direction confirmed by Jonathan, 2026-10-20. Design specification; not yet implemented in Lantern Studio or validated for fabrication.

## Customer receives

- Five CNC-cut wood panels: front, back, left, right, and bottom. The top is open toward the ceiling. This orientation is the working interpretation of five sides.
- Paper for the inside of all five panels, with attachment margins and an explicit cutting layout. Include the selected paper adhesive or mounting medium in the kit quote.
- A simple ceiling attachment kit, with any necessary mating pieces and fasteners included in the price and assembly instructions.
- Numbered parts and illustrated assembly instructions. No customer workshop or CNC machine is required.

The five panels are the main enclosure parts; mounting hardware and consumables are additional kit items, not omitted costs. CU or its fabrication supplier cuts the wood before shipment.

## Construction direction

Use sheet wood with decorative openings and integral borders/ribs CNC-cut into each panel. The handmade Stepped Shoji reference supplies the visual language; individually gluing sticks is no longer the default manufacturing method for this ceiling product.

Use complementary locating tabs and slots as the initial joint candidate. They must prevent misorientation, align adjacent faces, and allow an achievable assembly sequence. A locating joint is not automatically a locking joint: specify glue or mechanical retention explicitly. Provide a removable whole enclosure or another defined service route for lamp access.

Do not promise a perfect fit from nominal CAD dimensions alone. The manufacturing profile must record measured sheet thickness, cutter diameter, process variation, and tested joint allowance. Router-cut internal corners need tool-radius accommodation, such as suitable reliefs. Laser cutting is a separate process profile and cannot silently reuse router compensation. Produce a small joint coupon before manufacturing the five full panels.

The first build should use a simple rectangular envelope and repeatable panel patterns. Preserve the stepped silhouette where it can be cut as a single connected panel without fragile unsupported features. Every border and rib must remain connected to the panel; enclosed scrap must not become an accidental loose part.

## Ceiling attachment

A low-cost removable adhesive mount is a user-requested candidate, not a selected or approved component. Selection requires documented suitability for overhead use on the actual ceiling finish, complete assembly weight, and the intended attachment geometry. A wall-hanging rating does not establish ceiling suitability. Generic substitutes must be assessed individually; do not assume equivalence from appearance or price.

The photo reference appears to show a textured ceiling; surface suitability is unverified. Keep mounting status unresolved until a compatible attachment is identified. Investigate a compatible existing fixture attachment as another candidate without assuming the exposed stem is suitable. Do not generate a purchase-ready kit with the mount missing or priced at zero.

## Engine contract

Inputs: measured fixture envelope, desired finished size, panel material and actual thickness, manufacturing process and tool size, tested fit allowance, minimum rib width, decorative pattern, paper dimensions and margins, attachment selection, and assembly mass evidence.

Outputs: five uniquely identified panel drawings; CNC exports with declared units, outlines, pockets/through cuts and process settings; assembled preview; paper cut layout; joint and assembly map; complete bill of materials; estimated mass; and itemized quote behind the headline total.

Quote includes sheet usage and waste, CNC setup/cutting, paper, adhesive, mounting parts, packaging, and handling. Apply the existing 10% CU fee consistently; retain shipping/tax and unquoted supplier costs as unknowns where applicable. Do not reuse the current stick-built material estimate for this panel kit.

## Acceptance checks before a fitted prototype

1. Exactly five enclosure panels, with matching joints and no collisions in the assembled model.
2. Measured fixture fits the interior; dimensions account for panel thickness and assembly allowances.
3. All cut features are reachable with the selected tool and leave connected, manufacturable panels.
4. Paper covers every intended window with usable bonding margins; it does not obstruct joints or mounting interfaces.
5. Assembly order works without forcing or trapping the final panel; service removal is documented.
6. Joint coupon and an unlit full-size assembly physically fit; finished mass is measured.
7. Ceiling attachment is selected against its actual use conditions before an installation-ready kit is offered.

Photos establish style, not dimensions. Ceiling mounting, CNC generation, and this five-panel bill of materials remain new work; this document does not claim those app features exist.
