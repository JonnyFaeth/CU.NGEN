# Lower-cost cutting and machine ownership

Research date: 2026-10-20. Estimates, not quotes. Current sample has a synthetic 100 mm fixture projection. No additional supplier contacted or booking made.

## Access and service shortlist

- [PDX Hackerspace FAQ](https://pdxhackerspace.org/FAQ.html): $40/month membership, application and equipment sign-off. Its [laser wiki](https://wiki.pdxhackerspace.org/en/lasercutting/Overview) lists $5 per 30 minutes actual firing time and $5 monthly member credit. Confirm current laser availability, usable bed >=474.05 mm on both axes, stock acceptance and CU commercial prototyping policy before joining. Membership approval may take weeks. Public open houses do not themselves authorize machine use.
- [St. Helens Library](https://www.sthelensoregon.gov/1325/Makerspace): equipment access free, no residency or library-card requirement; some materials charged, outside stock subject to approval, orientation/certification required. Published equipment list names Glowforge Plus HD. [Glowforge printable area](https://support.glowforge.com/hc/en-us/articles/21251502058267-printable-area) is 11 x 19.5 inches: walls/coupons fit, one-piece bottom does not. A split bottom requires new joints and validation; it changes the five-piece requirement.
- [West Linn The Hive](https://westlinnoregon.gov/library/hive-makerspace): lists Glowforge Pro, mandatory training and booked one-hour sessions. Its page does not establish free material or equipment pricing. Pro passthrough potentially accommodates our bottom; confirm staff permit and support it, project eligibility, fees and time allowance. [Manufacturer passthrough documentation](https://support.glowforge.com/hc/en-us/articles/360047669153-Pro-Passthrough). Not a verified free full-kit solution.
- [Ponoko wood cutting](https://www.ponoko.com/laser-cutting/wood): advertises wood parts from $50, no minimum quantity. Its general laser page advertises $0 setup. These are not our kit price. Confirm stock-specific usable dimensions and material in quotation.
- [SendCutSend](https://sendcutsend.com/services/sheet-cutting/): no minimum quantity; machine process assigned automatically. Wood may be routed, which our current files do not support without internal-corner redesign. Verify laser assignment before ordering. [Shipping](https://sendcutsend.com/shipping/) advertises free US shipping on qualifying orders >=$39; this is not a $39 kit quote.
- [Digital Craft PDX](https://www.digitalcraftpdx.com/laser-cutting/): $65 job minimum, but its published 18-inch bed width cannot accommodate our current bottom. Redesign required or quote a different process.

## Transparent owned-machine scenario

This estimates five cut wooden panels and five loose paper inserts, NOT a complete mounted kit. Fixture hardware remains unresolved. No new purchase recommendation is implied.

Current design.json cut length: 27,682.933 mm excluding coupons. The PDX laser wiki suggests 10-20 mm/s for 1/8-inch wood as an example, not a transferable machine setting. Length / speed / 60 gives 23.07-46.14 minutes beam time. Plan approximately 1-1.5 operator hours for loading, checks, attended cutting, light cleanup and paper preparation. First-time learning, failed coupons and redesign can take longer.

Budget checkout separately. $4,000 installed is an illustrative assumption, not a supplier quote: extraction/venting, space, software, delivery and setup can change it materially. Optical laser wattage is not total electrical consumption. A smaller 16-inch bed does not fit our current bottom.

At PDX's published firing-time rate, 23-46 minutes corresponds to about $3.85-$7.69 if billed proportionally; confirm billing increments, coupon time and minimums. Add membership, materials, training and travel; the monthly $5 credit may offset some machine time. This is more promising for prototyping than buying equipment before validating the product.
