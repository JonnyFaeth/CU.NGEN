## Mount decision

Generic removable wall strips are not selected for overhead suspension. We need an exact product rated for the relevant ceiling surface, load direction and finished kit weight. Multiple hooks do not automatically provide the sum of their individual ratings.

The official [3M Command Ceiling Hooks 17008ES page](https://www.command.com.hk/3M/en_HK/p/d/v100777831/) gives a 0.5 lb (about 227 g) capacity and excludes popcorn/heavily textured ceilings. It describes lightweight decorative uses. The photographed ceiling has visible texture; its compatibility is unverified. These hooks are not approved for this wooden kit. This is not a claim that every Command product prohibits ceilings.

The first mechanical concept to investigate is the existing shade-retaining center stem: compatible coupling/extension, a reinforced load-spreading center pad, and a retaining washer/finial. This could avoid new ceiling holes. It is conditional on identifying the fixture, thread and permitted shade load, and checking the complete load path. Do not assume a lamp thread size from a photograph or substitute plumbing threads.

The existing bottom design has an 8 mm cross rib. Do not drill a mounting hole into that rib. A stem-mounted version needs a redesigned center pad and validated attachment of the walls to the supported bottom. Added mounting parts and reinforcement are not in the current quotation geometry.

Before selecting hardware: confirm model/thread, measure ceiling-to-lowest-point projection, weigh the completed prototype including paper/glue/hardware, and establish the fixture manufacturer's allowable support. Bench-test fit and retention before overhead installation. No adhesive mount SKU, installation procedure or hardware price is validated yet.
