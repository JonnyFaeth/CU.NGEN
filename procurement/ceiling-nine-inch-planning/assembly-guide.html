Assembly

  1. Have the supplier measure the actual sheet and cut the fit coupon with the intended kerf compensation. Test the tongue in the slot at 90 degrees. Adjust thickness/allowance and regenerate all files if loose or forced.
  2. Cut internal windows and slots before outer profiles. Label the pieces A–E on removable tape; SVG titles/IDs are metadata, not engraving paths.
  3. Dry-fit C and D between A and B: side end-tabs enter the four slots in each front/back panel. Assemble the four-wall ring before lowering it onto E. Do not try sliding corner tabs after the bottom is seated.
  4. Align the eight bottom tabs with E's eight slots and lower the complete ring evenly. Check dimensions, squareness and fixture envelope before glue.
  5. Disassemble. Cut the five paper rectangles using the paper layout; each overlaps its window envelope by 6 mm. Bond to the inside of each face, leaving all joints and slots uncovered. Allow the selected paper adhesive to dry.
  6. Reassemble with appropriate wood adhesive at the mating wood joints; keep paper clear of glue joints. Hold square and follow the adhesive manufacturer's cure instructions. The tabs locate the parts; they are not snap locks.
  7. Weigh and inspect the finished enclosure. The open top faces the ceiling. Installation and whole-enclosure removal for service require a separately verified attachment; no adhesive ceiling mount is specified in this package.
