Submission record, 2026-10-20: a revised version of this enquiry was successfully submitted through the supplier contact form. Subject: Quote request — five laser-cut plywood lantern panels, prototype and small batch

Hello MC Laser Labs,

I am developing CU.NGEN, a configurable paper-and-wood lantern enclosure. Could you quote the attached sample geometry in laser-compatible birch plywood, provisionally nominal 1/8 inch?

Please price one set of A/B/C/D/E plus one pair of fit coupons, and separately 5 and 10 enclosure sets. A/B are identical, as are C/D. SVG and DXF are alternate formats for the same parts. Units are mm; the largest part E is 474.05 mm square. Your published 32 × 20 inch bed appears large enough; please confirm usable cutting margins and stock availability.

Please include:

- Material species/grade, actual thickness and thickness variation; low-cost suitable alternatives.
- Material, setup, cutting, coupon and any file preparation charges, with minimum charges identified.
- Whether calibrated kerf compensation can produce these finished boundaries and a 0.15 mm total slot allowance; recommend a fit allowance if different.
- Prototype coupon-first process before cutting the whole set.
- Any geometry, charring, sanding or minimum-feature concerns, including the 8 mm ribs.
- Local pickup availability, lead time, quote validity, and any taxes or other charges.
- Optional separate price for cutting five paper inserts to the rectangles in design.json, if you can supply suitable diffuser paper.

This is a non-binding sample quotation request, not manufacturing authorization. Actual fixture projection and stock thickness will be confirmed before final drawings. Ceiling mounting is outside this cutting request and has not been validated. Please do not proceed with paid work.
