# Decision record

## 2026-10-04 — CU.NGEN foundation

- CU.NGEN means Custom Engine and is the umbrella for CU Homie and CU LeRobot.
- Optimize for total reproducible cost subject to task and reliability requirements.
- The first CU robot direction is a desktop cleaner; wheels are accepted.
- Users should not need a workshop or printer. CU may provide assembly as a future paid service.
- Self-charging is a required objective, not yet demonstrated.
- Requested furniture transitions have 5–15 cm height differences and a horizontal gap of unknown width.
- Vacuum anchoring is under investigation, not a selected or validated mechanism.
- CU Homie's existing implementation must be inventoried before describing or importing it.
- Public project creation does not establish a hardware design, product launch, or affiliation with Hugging Face.

## Superseded early direction

Earlier notes considered a two-legged walking humanoid and HopeJR as a default architecture. The accepted wheeled desktop-cleaning direction supersedes those requirements. HopeJR remains a research reference, not a selected full-body design.
